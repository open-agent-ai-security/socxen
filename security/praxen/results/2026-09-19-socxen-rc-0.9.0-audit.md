## Remit feedback

### R-34: over-broad bundled clause
- **Rule text (verbatim):** "Free-text field values, raw tool arguments, raw tool results, and neutralized hostile payloads MUST NEVER be written into the audit log, so that the audit trail cannot become a second copy of the evidence or of the attacker's content."
- **Defect class:** Two distinct obligations are bundled in one clause. Banning free text, raw results and hostile payloads is sound. But "raw tool arguments", read literally, also bans behavior the docs describe as routine: copying selected argument values (alertId, caseId, alertStatus, stage, disposition, …) into the audit record, which R-69 in fact requires.
- **Doc citation:** `plugin/docs/logging.md:42-50` (the decision record copies `action.alertId`, `action.alertStatus`, `action.disposition` from the gated write's arguments); `plugin/docs/logging.md:68-76` (the gate log line carries `"target": {"alertId": "4471", "alertStatus": "DISMISSED"}`); `plugin/docs/logging.md:110-111` frames the ban as "Tool arguments and results **in general** — `tool_args` / `tool_result` are always `null`".
- **Findings it drives:** PRAX-2026-09-19-006. That finding still stands under the narrower wording, because `recipients` is outside the bridge's allowlist. The feedback does not overturn it.
- **Narrower obligation the docs support:** "Whole tool-argument or tool-result payloads, free-text field values, and neutralized hostile payloads MUST NEVER be written into the audit log; only a fixed allowlist of identifier and state/disposition-enum fields may be recorded from a call's arguments, and every log that records call targets MUST use that same allowlist."

No defect found in the other cited rules: R-43 (matches `triage-cases/SKILL.md:139-141`), R-62 (conditional on detection, and it does not require the semantic detector that `input-canonicalizer.md:79` rules out), R-28 (matches `SECURITY.md:38-41`), R-69, R-72 (matches the gate's own documented disclosure, `logging.md:71`), R-24 (a deny-by-default or verb-pattern deny for unknown rule-write tools is feasible and does not conflict with any documented feature), and R-55. None is a heading-as-rule extraction.

BEHAVIOR SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
socxen's dominant pattern is deterministic enforcement at the two chokepoints
it owns: the bundled PreToolUse hook turns every disposition change, outbound
email, containment tool and rule write into a host-enforced ask or deny, and
the bridge wraps every read in fail-closed Unicode screening and every write
in fail-closed neutralization, state-only updates and a close-by-create guard.
No telemetry-to-action chain survives those gates, so the residual gaps sit
where the hook cannot see intent. The main one is the queue sweep: create_case
and create_case_notes are prompt-free for every skill and the hook carries no
skill identity, so the sweep's no-write rule rests on the prompt. Around it
sit smaller supply-chain and observability gaps: the runtime launch does not
enforce the hash-pinned lock, and the audit trail omits closedReason, turns
off silently, and copies mail recipients into the gate log.
